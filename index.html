How to play

A chef holds a tray above his head while pizza dough and slices fall from above. Hold the left or right half of the screen to move the chef and catch every piece; each catch is one point. The pieces stack into a growing pile that you have to keep balanced. If a piece falls past the tray or off the pile, the run ends. Later drops fall faster, bounce off round bumpers and sometimes come in pairs. Your best and last score are stored on your device. Use the speaker button to toggle sound and the pause button to take a break.
